# Fast Aircon Service Singapore Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Fast Aircon Service Singapore provides aircon servicing, maintenance, repairs, and installation for households and businesses in Singapore. The proposed homepage would help visitors choose a service and contact the team.

## Main Improvements

1. **Explain each service clearly.** The proposed layout would give general servicing, chemical wash, chemical overhaul, refrigerant top-up, installation, and repairs distinct sections. Short descriptions would explain when each option is appropriate so visitors can choose where to start.
2. **Make the brand recognisable at a glance.** The proposed hero would use the existing blue and cyan palette, snowflake symbol, and airflow motif to give the homepage a clearer visual identity.
3. **Make enquiries easier on mobile.** A prominent Call button and clear enquiry links beside service descriptions would help visitors contact the team from the section they are browsing. WhatsApp and email details need verification before they can be published.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service headings and short descriptions would make the page easier to scan. The blue and cyan palette would carry through the hero and enquiry buttons, helping visitors recognise key actions as they scroll.
